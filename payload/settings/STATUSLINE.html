Your status line

One line at the bottom of Claude Code tells you which account you are on, which model is working, how full the conversation is, how much of your limits is used, and where you are.

Your line, left to right

Work1 | rc2 | Opus 5.5 · high3 | 182.0k / 1.0M · 3.2MB · 0 compacts4 | 5h 63% · ↻ 2h14m5 | 7d 18% · ↻ 4d6h6 | demo ⎇ feature/login7

The numbers match the cards below. Parts hide themselves when their data is missing, so your line can be shorter than this one.

Colors at a glance

  • green = fine
  • yellow = keep an eye on it
  • orange = getting full, or strong model/effort
  • red = act now, or strongest
